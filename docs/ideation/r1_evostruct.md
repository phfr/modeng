## Step 1 — eight assumptions of transfer-LightGBM and the baselines, and what follows if each is false

| # | Assumption | If false |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **A gene is an atomic identifier.** Transfer reads only the gene's own column of the disease x gene matrix (`_transfer`); two genes share nothing unless a disease lists both. | Then zero-support genes (found 0.4 % of the time, brief 3) are a representational artefact: a gene-gene kernel K built from evidence that exists for *every* gene (homology, co-evolution, shared fold) turns the donor matrix L into L·K and gives every empty column borrowed support. |
| A2 | **The donor universe is the set of human diseases in Open Targets.** | Then other "disease x gene" matrices can be stacked under L as extra donor rows: model-organism phenotypes, livestock/dog trait loci, lineage-specific rate shifts across mammals. Seed overlap picks the relevant rows automatically, no manual phenotype mapping. These rows are populated for genes with no human disease at all (brief 3: the unreachable pool). |
| A3 | **The gene is the right unit of association.** | Then the transferable unit may be a domain, a homologous residue position or an interface. A gene that has never been a disease gene can still carry a domain whose homologous positions are pathogenic in five paralogs. |
| A4 | **Network edges are homogeneous and undirected**, so the only usable signal is "how many neighbours", i.e. degree (brief 2: GNN = MLP). | Then the network did not fail because interactions are irrelevant but because edge *identity* was missing: which interface, and whether selection / pathogenic variation sits on that interface. Edge-specific evidence could rescue it. |
| A5 | **Gene covariates act the same way in every disease** (one global propensity; LightGBM mostly learns "constrained, well-studied gene = more likely"). | Then constraint is a disease-specific *mechanism signature* (haploinsufficient developmental genes vs LoF-tolerant recessive enzymes vs fast-evolving immune genes), and matching the seeds' signature is informative within the unknown-gene pool where the global prior only reaches AUC 0.60-0.63 (brief 3). |
| A6 | **Future positives are drawn from the same process that generated the current labels**, so learning P(gene in disease) from past associations is the right objective. | 82 % of strictly-new positives are GWAS-only power events (brief 4). Then the target is P(true) x P(becomes detectable at the next sample size), and the second factor is governed by selection (effect size x allele frequency), which constraint metrics estimate. |
| A7 | **Available evidence about a gene is proportional to how much it was studied** (labels, PPI, GO, KG, text all share one study bias; nuAP has to correct for it afterwards). | Sequence-derived evolutionary evidence is computed uniformly for all 19,255 genes. If it carries module signal at all, its nuAP/AP ratio should be much higher than that of any literature-derived input, and it should be the one input that still works for genes unknown to any disease. |
| A8 | **Unlabelled = negative, and one disease = one homogeneous module.** | If modules are mixtures of mechanism classes (e.g. one complex + one metabolic pathway + GWAS regulatory hits), a single Jaccard similarity to the whole seed set dilutes each sub-module; per-sub-module transfer and per-arrival-class scoring would beat the pooled ranking. (Not developed further here: decomposition is covered by subex in `banned_r1.md`.) |

## Step 2 — ideas

Common design: every idea is expressed either as extra donor rows (A2) or as a gene-gene / gene-unit kernel applied to the donor matrix (A1, A3), so
it reuses `_transfer` including its exact family-mask correction, and is reported **alone, added to `transfer_jaccard`, and added to `modet_headline`**
with the paired cluster bootstrap. All tests are CPU-only. Additional stratum read-out for every idea (computable from `B.hidden_counts` / the context
matrix): recall@100 for (a) zero-transfer-support targets and (b) targets whose column in the context is empty ("unknown to any disease").

### EV-1 — Phenolog donors: model-organism phenotypes as transfer rows
- **Mechanism.** Stack a phenotype x human-ortholog matrix (mouse MP, zebrafish ZP, fly, worm, yeast phenotypes) under the context matrix and let Jaccard-to-seeds pick donor rows, exactly as for human diseases. Non-obvious "phenologs" (McGary et al. 2010 PNAS: e.g. yeast phenotype gene sets that overlap human disease gene sets) need no phenotype mapping; the seed overlap is the mapping. Gives transfer support to genes with zero human disease association.
- **p_typical:** 0.30. New reason vs the banned "mouse KO oracle" (idea only, never run): not a per-gene feature or oracle but donor rows inside the dominant mechanism, across five species, with row-specific weights.
- **Data.** On disk: `/mnt/msadb/modet/downloads/impc_release-13.0/results_geneAndMPTermAssociation.csv.gz` (7.8 MB dir, ~7.3k MP term x zygosity rows) and `ensembl103_compara` homologies (143 MB) for orthologs. Download for the other species: Monarch KG gene-to-phenotype dump, `https://data.monarchinitiative.org/monarch-kg/<date>/tsv/all_associations/` (file `gene_to_phenotypic_feature_association.all.tsv.gz`; whole graph `monarch-kg.tar.gz`, a few GB; dated snapshots from 2024 were listed, current file names not checked).
- **Leakage.** IMPC 13.0 + Compara 103 only: **temporal-clean** (both are in modet's T0 downloads). With a Monarch 2024+ dump: **transductive** on `temporal` (phenotype curation after 2021-04, and mice are often phenotyped *because* a human gene was found), fair on `forward` if the snapshot is <= 2025-09. Main route: human-gene-triggered model-organism curation; second route: Monarch files mix human HPO rows — must filter to non-human taxa.
- **Minimal test (<= 30 min).** Build `P_impc` [n_MPterms, 19,255] via mouse->human one2one/one2many orthologs; `score = _transfer` on `vstack([B.context, P_impc])` with phenotype rows never masked (they hold no hidden pairs) and a single scalar weight for phenotype rows tuned on half of dev. `python -m modeng.bench --method ev1_phenolog,ev1_phenolog_plus_transfer --bench temporal --split dev`. Runtime: seconds.
- **Expected.** nuAP +0.004 to +0.010 over `transfer_jaccard`, about +0.003 over headline; AP ~0. Zero-support recall@100 from 0.4 % to 2-3 % (IMPC 13.0 covers only ~7k genes, most already known to some disease). **Kill:** paired delta nuAP vs transfer < +0.004 on temporal dev AND zero-support recall@100 < 1.5 %.
- **Most likely failure.** OT already ingests IMPC as an evidence source, so the mouse rows mostly restate what human disease rows carry; the gain is confined to the non-mouse species, which cover old, well-studied genes.

### EV-2 — Homology-lifted transfer through paralogs and shared folds
- **Mechanism.** Replace L by L·K where K links each gene to its paralogs (Compara, weighted by a decreasing function of dS / duplication age) and to members of its AFDB structural cluster; then run transfer. A gene with an empty column inherits the disease profile of its family, so "the next gene of a disease family" (e.g. another channel subunit, another collagen) receives disease-specific support instead of none. K is learned as two or three scalars (paralog age bins, fold co-membership), not as features.
- **p_typical:** 0.25. New reason vs the banned "smoothing over network" (idea only): the kernel is sequence-derived, has no study-bias degree, and is applied to the donor matrix (disease-specific), not to output scores.
- **Data.** On disk: `ensembl103_compara/Compara.103.protein_default.homologies.tsv.gz` (has `homology_type`, `identity`, `dn`, `ds`; whether human within-species paralog rows are in this file must be checked — the two rows I read were orthologs; fallback: Ensembl BioMart paralog table, ~20 MB), `/mnt/msadb/subex/raw/afdb_clusters_v3` (46 MB), `modet/data/processed/features/esmc.parquet` as an alternative kernel (cosine kNN).
- **Leakage.** **temporal-clean** (Ensembl 103 = Feb 2021; AFDB clusters are sequence/structure-derived, published 2023 but contain no disease knowledge — declare as "post-T0 tool, label-free"). Main route: none through labels; a subtle one is that L·K must be computed from `B.context_for(task)`, otherwise a hidden target's own column leaks back through its paralog's lift into family rows.
- **Minimal test (<= 45 min).** `K = I + a*P_young + b*P_old + c*F_fold` (row-normalised), `score = _transfer(L @ K)`; grid 4x4x3 on half of dev, evaluate on the other half and on `forward` dev. Runtime: minutes (sparse products).
- **Expected.** nuAP +0.005 to +0.012 over transfer, +0.003 to +0.006 over headline; AP -0.002 to +0.003 (paralog families are not hubs). Zero-support recall@100 to ~3 %. **Kill:** delta nuAP vs transfer < +0.004 on both temporal and forward dev.
- **Most likely failure.** Paralogs diverge in tissue and disease more often than they share it; the lift adds many false positives per true one and only helps Mendelian families, which are a minority of arrivals (57 % are GWAS-only, brief 4).

### EV-3 — Co-evolution kernel: a study-bias-free interactome
- **Mechanism.** Build a gene-gene kernel from clade-wise phylogenetic-profile correlation (CladeOScope NPP) and mammalian evolutionary rate covariation (ERC2.0, 120 mammals), and use it (i) as the lift kernel of EV-2 and (ii) as the network for the existing RWR baseline. Unlike STRING it has an entry for every gene pair and its "degree" is unrelated to publication count, so the brief-2 result "network = degree" may not apply (A7).
- **p_typical:** 0.20 (phylogenetic profiling is a textbook suggestion; using it as a degree-free replacement network specifically to test the "network = degree" finding, and ERC across 120 mammals, is less common).
- **Data.** On disk: `/mnt/msadb/subex/raw/cladeoscope/9606_NPP.tsv` (387 MB, 20,191 x 1,028, pre-T0 per its README). Download: ERC2.0 matrices, Dryad `https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.6m905qg8q` (7.54 GB zip, dated 2025-03-13; file layout inside not checked; Dryad may need a browser session, as it did for Zhang 2025).
- **Leakage.** NPP: **temporal-clean**. ERC2.0: label-free, derived from genome sequences only; post-T0 publication, so report as "temporal-clean (label-free post-T0 resource)" and show the NPP-only number beside it. Main route: essentially none; the risk is a confound, not leakage (co-evolution ~ co-expression level ~ essentiality).
- **Minimal test (<= 1.5 h, NPP only).** Per-clade z-scored NPP -> top-50 mutual kNN graph (dense 20k x 20k correlation, ~3 GB float32, ~2 min on 32 cores) -> register as network `npp_knn50` -> run existing `rwr` with it, and EV-2's lift with it. First check, 5 min: correlation between kNN degree and STRING degree / gene2pubmed count (should be < 0.2, otherwise the premise is gone).
- **Expected.** Alone: nuAP 0.02-0.04 (above RWR's 0.01-0.02 because no hub penalty), AP low. On top of transfer: +0.003 to +0.008 nuAP; on top of headline: +0.000 to +0.004. **Kill:** alone nuAP < 0.02 on temporal dev OR delta over transfer < +0.003.
- **Most likely failure.** Co-evolution recovers ancient core machinery (ribosome, proteasome, cilia, mitochondrial complexes); disease modules outside those are too recent or too regulatory to leave a co-evolution trace. Cilia / mitochondrial diseases would improve and nothing else.

### EV-4 — Selection-aware discovery hazard for GWAS-only arrivals
- **Mechanism.** Model an arrival as P(causal) x P(detectable at the next sample size) (A6). Under stabilizing selection, strongly constrained genes carry their signal at low frequency and small common-variant effect, so they cross significance later; score = own-trait sub-threshold MAGMA z combined with gene constraint (s_het / LOEUF) in a two-parameter hazard, and within a sub-threshold locus rank genes by constraint. The disease-specific part is the z; the evolutionary part says which sub-threshold genes are true and late rather than noise.
- **p_typical:** 0.30 (sub-threshold z is flagged in brief 4; the constraint x z interaction and the within-locus constraint choice are the added part). Not the banned "tier-0 gene-propensity prior": constraint enters only as a modifier of a disease-specific signal.
- **Data.** On disk: `modet/data/processed/gwas_magma_t0/` (4.4 GB, per-study `_w0/_w10/_leads.parquet`), `gnomad_v2.1.1/gnomad.v2.1.1.lof_metrics.by_gene.txt.bgz`. Optional download: GeneBayes s_het, Zenodo `https://zenodo.org/records/10403680` (record ~6.5 GB; only the per-gene posterior table is needed; published 2023-12).
- **Leakage.** With gnomAD v2.1.1 + T0 MAGMA: **temporal-clean**. With GeneBayes s_het: label-free post-T0 resource (it uses gene features incl. expression and PPI embeddings as a prior — check that no disease annotation is among them before calling it clean). Main route: the study -> disease mapping; a T0 study must be the trait's own pre-2021-04 GWAS, never a later one.
- **Minimal test (<= 1 h).** For temporal dev diseases that have a mapped T0 study (count unknown to me; brief 4 reports the AUC ~0.8 result for 4 diseases only), `score = transfer + w1*z + w2*z*constraint`, fit w on half. Read out on the GWAS-only arrival class (modet `groundtruth/` arrival classes). Runtime: minutes.
- **Expected.** On covered diseases: GWAS-only recall@100 from 16 % (brief 4) to 22-25 %; nuAP +0.01 to +0.02 on those diseases, diluted to +0.002 to +0.006 over all dev diseases. Interaction term alone (w2) worth about a quarter of that. **Kill:** z x constraint term adds < +0.003 nuAP over transfer + z on covered diseases (then the evolutionary part is not needed and the idea belongs to another lens).
- **Most likely failure.** Too few dev diseases have a T0 own-trait GWAS with full summary statistics; and MAGMA z already absorbs gene length / SNP density, leaving little for constraint.

### EV-5 — Livestock and dog trait loci as orthologous power donors
- **Mechanism.** Domestic-animal genetics is an independent, very highly powered replicate of mammalian complex-trait mapping (cattle stature, milk fat, fertility; pig backfat; dog body size, breed-specific disease) plus hundreds of Mendelian animal disorders (OMIA). Map candidate genes of each animal QTL trait / OMIA phene to human orthologs and add them as donor rows; a human GWAS gene that has not yet reached significance (the 57 % stratum, brief 4) may already be a mapped QTL gene in a species with different LD, different allele frequencies and strong artificial selection.
- **p_typical:** 0.04.
- **Data.** Download: Animal QTLdb (`https://www.animalgenome.org/QTLdb`; per-species QTL/association tables with candidate genes, < 1 GB; exact files and licence not checked) and OMIA (`https://omia.org`, gene-phene table, a few MB; not checked). Orthologs: `ensembl103_compara` on disk.
- **Leakage.** Animal QTLdb / OMIA releases are dated; a release <= 2021-04 would be **temporal-clean**, the current one is **transductive** on `temporal` and fair on `forward` (if <= 2025-09). Main route: candidate genes at animal QTLs are often *named by authors because of the human literature* (the human gene is known, so the cattle paper picks it) — this is a circularity that inflates the result; mitigate by using positional gene sets (all genes within the QTL interval) instead of author-nominated candidates.
- **Minimal test (<= 2 h incl. download).** OMIA + cattle/pig/dog QTLdb -> trait x human-ortholog matrix (positional variant and author-candidate variant) -> donor rows as in EV-1. Compare positional vs author-candidate rows: a large gap = circularity. Runtime after download: minutes.
- **Expected.** +0.002 to +0.006 nuAP over transfer overall; concentrated in anthropometric, lipid, metabolic, skeletal, eye and skin diseases (maybe 10-15 dev diseases, +0.01 to +0.03 there); AP ~0. GWAS-only recall@100 +1 to +2 points. **Kill:** positional-row variant gives < +0.002 nuAP over transfer and < 5 dev diseases improve by > 0.01.
- **Most likely failure.** Animal QTL intervals are megabases wide (long LD in breeds), so positional rows are nearly random gene sets, and the author-nominated rows are circular.

### EV-6 — Convergent-evolution trait modules across mammals as donors
- **Mechanism.** Treat the mammalian phylogeny as hundreds of natural experiments: for each lineage trait (subterranean / vision loss, marine, hairlessness, body size, longevity, diet, hibernation, echolocation, high altitude) compute per-gene association of relative evolutionary rate or gene loss with the trait (RERconverge-style), giving trait x gene donor rows; additionally use the raw gene x branch relative-rate matrix and regress it on the seed set ("in which lineages are the seeds jointly relaxed or accelerated?") to score all genes. Seeds of retinal disease select the mole/bat rows, seeds of skin disease the cetacean rows, without any curated mapping — and the evidence exists for genes no human paper has touched.
- **p_typical:** 0.05.
- **Data.** Download: ERC2.0 Dryad archive (7.54 GB, see EV-3) if it contains per-branch relative rates for the 120 mammals (not checked); otherwise TOGA orthology / loss calls for 488 placental mammals (Kirilenko et al. 2023 Science, doi 10.1126/science.abn3107; download location not found in my search) and a trait table (PanTHERIA / AnAge, a few MB). Fallback with data on disk and no download: dN/dS per species from `ensembl103_compara` (`dn`, `ds` columns) gives a gene x ~100-species rate matrix.
- **Leakage.** **temporal-clean** in substance (genome sequences + species traits, no human disease knowledge; resources published after 2021 are label-free). Main route: none through labels. Confound to control: overall rate (constraint) — work with rate *residuals* after removing each gene's mean and each branch's mean.
- **Minimal test (<= 2 h, on-disk fallback).** From Compara: matrix R[gene, species] = log(dN/dS) for one2one orthologs (NULLs present — I saw NULL dn/ds in the first row; expect usable values mainly for mammals/vertebrates), double-centre, impute by gene mean. Method A: `score = R_c @ ridge_fit(R_c[seeds])`-style seed-profile correlation (one line: cosine between each gene's residual profile and the seeds' mean residual profile). Method B: kNN kernel on R_c into EV-2's lift. Runtime: reading the 143 MB gz ~5-10 min, rest seconds.
- **Expected.** Alone nuAP 0.01-0.02; over transfer +0.002 to +0.006 overall, with a visible effect (+0.02 to +0.05) on a handful of sensory, skin, skeletal, immune and reproductive diseases. Zero-support recall@100 to ~1.5-2 %. **Kill:** seed-profile correlation alone has per-disease AUROC < 0.55 on average on temporal dev (i.e. no signal beyond chance once mean rate is removed).
- **Most likely failure.** Per-gene branch rates are noisy (short genes, few substitutions), and Ensembl pairwise dN/dS to human is not a per-branch rate; the proper per-branch matrix needs the Dryad / TOGA data, which may not arrive within the night.

### EV-7 — Interface-resolved modules: which edge, not how many
- **Mechanism.** Use predicted complex structures to turn each PPI edge into an interface (residue sets on both sides), then weight the edge seed -> candidate by the *excess* missense intolerance of the interface residues over the rest of the protein surface (AlphaMissense per-residue mean, or T0 ClinVar pathogenic vs gnomAD density), on both partners. Propagate seeds only over edges whose interface is under such focused constraint; this gives the network the edge-specific information whose absence would explain "network = degree" (A4).
- **p_typical:** 0.08.
- **Data.** On disk: `/mnt/msadb/subex/raw/burke2023_huintaf2/41594_2022_910_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx` (14 MB; Table S1: 65,484 AF2 pair models with pDockQ and IF_plDDT; needs `openpyxl`, which is missing in the base env). Interface residues are NOT in the table: they need the model coordinates (figshare 16866202, 15.3 GB, not downloaded per the README). Download: AlphaMissense `AlphaMissense_aa_substitutions.tsv.gz` from `https://zenodo.org/records/10813168` (~1.2 GB from memory, size not confirmed; licence stated inconsistently by mirrors — check the record). Not available: Zhang 2025 predictions (`zhang2025_humanppi/README.txt`: not downloaded).
- **Leakage.** Structures: label-free (AF2 weights PDB <= 2018). AlphaMissense: published 2023, trained on population frequency and primate variation, calibrated/evaluated on ClinVar -> report as **transductive** on `temporal` unless the T0 ClinVar/gnomAD v2 density variant is used; fair on `forward` and `holdout`. Main route: residue pathogenicity scores correlating with post-2021 ClinVar submissions for the candidate gene itself (a gene-level "is a disease gene" signal smuggled in through its mean score) — hence the *excess over the gene's own surface mean*, never the raw mean.
- **Minimal test (<= 2 h without the 15 GB download).** Coarse version: edge weight = pDockQ (>= 0.5) x per-gene whole-protein AlphaMissense mean excess is not interface-specific, so instead test the *premise* first: restrict RWR to the high-confidence Burke edges (pDockQ >= 0.5) vs a degree-matched random subset of the same HuRI/hu.MAP edges, and report nuAP. If structure-confident edges are not better than matched random edges, stop. Full version (needs figshare models, ~1 h to extract contacts at 8 A with 32 cores): interface-excess edge weights -> `rwr` with weighted adjacency. Runtime: minutes after extraction.
- **Expected.** Coverage is the limit: tens of thousands of edges over a few thousand genes. Alone nuAP ~0.01-0.02; over transfer +0.001 to +0.004; but on the covered targets (those with a confident interface to a seed) recall@100 could double relative to plain RWR. AP unchanged. **Kill:** interface-weighted RWR does not beat unweighted RWR on the same edge set by >= 20 % relative nuAP on temporal dev.
- **Most likely failure.** Too few seed-target pairs have a confident predicted interface, so even a strong per-edge effect is invisible at the benchmark level (below the 0.01 MDE, brief 7).

### EV-8 — Homologous-site transfer: donors at domain and residue level
- **Mechanism.** Change the unit of the donor matrix from gene to domain family (A3): build disease x domain-family associations from where the seeds' pathogenic variants fall (or, coarser, from seed domain content weighted by enrichment), then score every gene carrying that family by how intolerant *its own copy* of the domain is at the homologous positions (regional missense constraint / per-residue AlphaMissense in that domain relative to the gene's background). A gene never linked to any disease gets a disease-specific score because its copy of a domain, pathogenic in paralogs for this disease family, is itself under focused purifying selection.
- **p_typical:** 0.07.
- **Data.** Domain annotation: UniProt Swiss-Prot 2021_02 on disk (`/mnt/msadb/modet/downloads/uniprot/uniprot_sprot-only2021_02.tar.gz`, Pfam/InterPro cross-references and domain coordinates are in the flat file; not opened by me). Per-residue constraint: AlphaMissense substitutions file (see EV-7) or gnomAD v2.1.1 exome sites (large; alternative: published regional-missense-constraint tables). Variant positions for the fine version: ClinVar archive snapshot <= 2021-04 (`https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pub/clinvar/` archive, ~100 MB; path not checked).
- **Leakage.** Coarse version (domain content + gnomAD v2 constraint): **temporal-clean**. With AlphaMissense: **transductive** on `temporal` (same reasoning as EV-7), fair on `forward`/`holdout`. Main route: in `holdout`, ClinVar variants of a hidden gene must not be used to build the disease x domain row (build it from seeds only — automatic if rows are built per task from `t.seeds`).
- **Minimal test (<= 1.5 h, coarse version, no download).** Parse Pfam cross-references from the Swiss-Prot flat file -> G [genes x Pfam families], IDF-weighted; `L_dom = B.context_for(task) @ G` (disease x family), task profile `s_dom = seeds @ G`; `score_g = sum_f G[g,f] * enrich(f | seeds, background) * c[g]` with c = gene missense z (`mis_z` in the gnomAD file) as the stand-in for domain-level constraint. Compare with and without c, and against EV-2 (if EV-8 <= EV-2, the domain unit adds nothing beyond paralogy). Runtime: minutes.
- **Expected.** Over transfer +0.004 to +0.010 nuAP; AP ~+0.002. Strongest in Mendelian arrivals and in rare diseases with 3-5 seeds (few seeds still define a domain signature). Unknown-to-any-disease pool: within-pool AUC from ~0.60-0.63 (brief 3) to ~0.66. **Kill:** within-pool AUC gain < 0.02 AND delta nuAP over transfer < +0.004.
- **Most likely failure.** Promiscuous domains (kinase, zinc finger, WD40, Ig) dominate and carry no disease specificity even after IDF; the residue-level version that would fix this needs variant positions for seeds, which most GWAS-defined seeds do not have.

### EV-9 — Mechanism-signature matching in "selection space" (meta-learned)
- **Mechanism.** Describe every gene by a short, purely evolutionary vector (LOEUF, missense z, s_het, pHaplo/pTriplo, gene age / phylostratum from the NPP matrix, paralog count and age, mean dN/dS in primates vs mammals, loss frequency across mammals) and learn *across diseases* a set function that maps a seed set's distribution in this space to a scoring function (hyper-network / deep-sets, trained on non-dev diseases). Different from a global prior and from the banned per-disease PoPS ridge: the per-disease weights are predicted from the seed set, so a 3-seed rare disease gets a regularised mechanism signature (A5), and the features contain no literature-derived signal.
- **p_typical:** 0.12.
- **Data.** On disk: gnomAD v2.1.1, CladeOScope NPP, Compara. Download: pHaplo/pTriplo (Collins et al. 2022 Cell, supplementary table, a few MB; URL not checked), GeneBayes s_het (Zenodo 10403680).
- **Leakage.** On-disk subset: **temporal-clean**. pHaplo/pTriplo and s_het were trained with gene features / known dosage-sensitive genes published after or around T0: **transductive** on `temporal`. Main route: supervised constraint scores trained on known disease genes (pHaplo uses known haploinsufficient genes as positives) — a direct label route; prefer the unsupervised ones.
- **Minimal test (<= 1.5 h).** 12-dim feature table; baseline = single global LightGBM on it; method = deep-sets (two 64-unit MLPs, CPU) trained leave-disease-cluster-out on context rows of non-task diseases with 80/20 seed/target resampling; add to transfer. Runtime: ~20 min CPU.
- **Expected.** Over transfer +0.002 to +0.005 nuAP; over headline (which already has gene covariates) +0.000 to +0.003; main visible effect in the unknown-gene pool (AUC +0.02 to +0.03). **Kill:** set-conditioned model does not beat the global model on the same features by >= +0.003 nuAP (then there is no disease-specific mechanism signal, only propensity).
- **Most likely failure.** Twelve constraint features are nearly one-dimensional ("constrained vs not"), so the conditional model collapses to the global prior that the headline already contains.

## Bets

**Would bet on: EV-2 + EV-8 as one object** ("lifted transfer": L -> L·K with K from paralogy, shared fold and shared constrained domain). It attacks the
single measured cause of failure (zero transfer support, brief 3) inside the single mechanism that works (transfer, brief 1), costs minutes, is
temporal-clean with on-disk data, and has a clean falsifier (zero-support recall@100). EV-1 and EV-3 are the same operation with other kernels / rows,
so one implementation (`_transfer` on a stacked, lifted matrix with a few learned scalars) tests four ideas. Honest prior: +0.005 to +0.012 nuAP over
transfer and about half of that over the headline — real but not a paradigm shift on the aggregate metric; the stratum read-outs are where it should show.

**Most underrated: EV-6 (with EV-5 as its sibling).** These are the only proposals here that bring in *new observations about gene-phenotype links that
no human study produced* — the phylogeny and animal breeding as natural experiments — and so are the only ones that could in principle reach the
"unknown to any disease" and GWAS-only strata without borrowing from existing human labels. Probability of a benchmark-level gain is low (my guess
~15-20 %), the on-disk fallback (pairwise dN/dS) is a weak proxy for per-branch rates, and EV-5 carries a circularity risk that must be tested with
positional gene sets; but a positive result would be a new information source rather than a new estimator.

**Caveats that apply to all of the above.** No number here is measured. I did not open the Burke table (missing `openpyxl`), did not verify that
Compara's file contains within-human paralog rows, did not check the internal layout of the ERC2.0 Dryad archive, and did not confirm download
paths for TOGA, Animal QTLdb, OMIA, ClinVar archive or pHaplo/pTriplo. Those are the first things to check before scheduling the tests.
