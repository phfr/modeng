## Step 1 — eight assumptions of transfer LightGBM and the baselines, and what follows if false

| # | Assumption | If it is false |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Association is symmetric.** `tr_max` uses overlap between disease gene sets; a donor's genes flow to the target as readily as the reverse. | Disease relations are directed (risk factor -> outcome, cause -> complication). Genes should flow along the causal arrow only; half the donor mass is noise, and the direction can be estimated (CI-04). |
| A2 | **A label is a property of the gene-disease pair**, not of the measurement process. | Labels = (causal) x (detected). 57 % of arrivals are GWAS-only and 82 % of strictly-new ones are power events (brief #4): the detection factor is separately modellable from study size, gene architecture and sub-threshold signal (CI-05, CI-06). |
| A3 | **Module genes are exchangeable**: one score, one notion of membership. | Omnigenic view: a few core genes (Mendelian/rare-variant/drug) plus many peripheral trans-regulators (GWAS). The two arrive through different channels and need different predictors: "is co-member of the core" vs "is upstream of the core" (CI-01, CI-02). |
| A4 | **Proximity in an undirected graph (PPI, KG, co-annotation) proxies mechanism.** | Measured: it does not beyond degree (brief #2). Directed, interventional edges (knock-down g changes seed expression; variant changes protein in trans) carry information that undirected co-membership cannot (CI-01, CI-02). |
| A5 | **A gene must already be "known to some disease" to be scored** (transfer support). | Zero-support positives are found 0.4 % of the time (brief #3). Genome-wide own-trait causal evidence (cis-MR, sub-threshold z, burden) scores every gene with an instrument regardless of prior disease knowledge (CI-03, CI-08, CI-09). |
| A6 | **The feature-label relation learned on past arrivals holds for future arrivals** (i.i.d. across eras, evidence channels, therapeutic areas). | Discovery technology shifts (array GWAS -> biobank GWAS -> exome burden). Features that work through ascertainment (popularity, degree) change sign or strength; only invariant predictors survive to `forward` and to the zero-support stratum (CI-07). |
| A7 | **One mixing rule for all diseases**: the same weight on transfer vs genetics vs network everywhere. | The right weight depends on where the disease sits on its own discovery curve (GWAS N now vs expected). A disease about to get a 10x GWAS should be ranked by its own sub-threshold signal; a rare Mendelian one by core-membership (CI-05). |
| A8 | **Evidence is unsigned and additive**: more association = more membership. | Causal evidence has direction and dose (LoF, missense, eQTL, drug). Concordant direction across independent instruments is much stronger than the sum of magnitudes; discordance flags LD/pleiotropy artefacts (CI-03, CI-09). |

## Step 2 — ideas

### CI-01 — Upstream-of-core: knock-downs that move the seed program
- **Mechanism.** Treat seeds as (noisy) core genes and score every perturbed gene g by how coherently its knock-down shifts the seeds' expression in Perturb-seq (signed mean z over seed readouts, against matched random readout sets), plus the transpose (g's readout responds to seed knock-downs). With own-trait gene z available, fit Ota-style program effects: regress trait z on perturbation-program loadings and score g by its loading x program effect (gene -> program -> trait). This is a directed, interventional edge, which undirected PPI/KG never supplied.
- **p_typical:** 0.15
- **Data.** On disk: `/mnt/msadb/subex/derived/ps_{k562_gwps,cd4_2025,cd4_2025_rest,cd4_2025_stim48hr,hepg2_nadig,jurkat_nadig,rpe1_ess}.npy` (+ `_genes.txt`). Method reference: Ota et al., Nature 2025, "Causal modelling of gene effects from regulators to programs to traits" (https://mattlee821.github.io/papers/genetics/ota_2025_41372418.html, press: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/12/251215084201.htm).
  **Leakage:** K562/RPE1 (2022), HepG2/Jurkat (2025), CD4 (2025) are all post-2021-04 -> `transductive` on `temporal`; clean on `forward` only if the release predates 2025-09 (check the CD4 and Nadig dates), `holdout-current` otherwise. Route: low, since these are unbiased genome-scale screens that do not encode disease knowledge; the realistic route is none beyond date formalism. Report on `forward` as the claim-bearing benchmark.
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~20 min).** `python -m modeng.run --bench forward --split dev --method ci01_upstream` where `score(tasks, ctx)`: load the 4 matrices once, z-score columns, for each task compute `S = M[:, seed_cols]`; score = |mean(S)| * sqrt(n_seed) minus the same statistic for 200 expression-matched random column sets; max over cell lines; genes not perturbed get the task median. Second variant: late-fuse rank-average with `transfer_jaccard`. Report all-genes and the zero-transfer-support stratum, and immune vs non-immune diseases (CD4 context).
- **Expected.** Alone nuAP ~0.02-0.03 overall (coverage 9.8k-12k perturbed genes; cell context matches only blood/immune/liver). Fused with transfer: +0.005-0.010 nuAP overall, AP +0.000-0.005; in immune/haematological diseases +0.02 nuAP; zero-support recall@100 from 0.4 % to 2-3 %. **Kill:** fused gain < +0.004 nuAP on dev AND zero-support stratum AUROC < 0.58 (the 0.60-0.63 ceiling in brief #3 is the bar to approach from a single source).
- **Most likely failure.** Wrong cell context for most diseases, and essential-gene knock-downs dominate the coherent-shift statistic (generic stress response looks "upstream of everything").

### CI-02 — Trans-QTL convergence: where the disease's own variants land
- **Mechanism.** Take the disease's known GWAS lead variants (seed loci, known at the seed date) and look up their trans-eQTL / trans-pQTL targets; genes hit in trans by many independent disease loci are convergent downstream (core) genes in the omnigenic sense. The scored gene needs no GWAS signal and no prior disease annotation of its own. Direction is from germline variant, so reverse causation is excluded.
- **p_typical:** 0.07
- **Data.** Seed loci: `ot_evidence/evidence_2104/sourceId=ot_genetics_portal` (temporal) and `evidence_2509/sourceId=gwas_credible_sets` (forward), `modet/data/processed/gwas_magma_t0/*_leads.parquet`. Download: eQTLGen phase 1 trans-eQTL (10,317 trait SNPs x 19,942 genes, blood, n=31,684; significant-only file ~60 MB, full ~10 GB; https://www.eqtlgen.org/trans-eqtls.html; preprint 2018, published 2021-09). UKB-PPP pQTL (2,923 proteins; Synapse syn51364943, account needed; clumped lead table is in the Nature 2023 supplement, a few MB; https://metabolomics.helmholtz-munich.de/ukbbpgwas/ , https://registry.opendata.aws/ukbppp/).
  **Leakage:** eQTLGen sig. file was public from the 2018 preprint -> `temporal-clean` on `temporal` (state the file date). UKB-PPP (2023) -> `transductive` on `temporal`, clean on `forward`. Route: eQTLGen tested only trait-associated SNPs chosen from the 2018 GWAS catalog (selection by disease knowledge, but pre-T0), and LD between a seed locus and a cis effect on a neighbouring gene (mask +-1 Mb around every seed locus).
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~40 min incl. download of the significant-only file).** `score`: map seed loci to rsIDs/positions (LD proxies r2>0.8 via the 1000G EUR panel already at `/mnt/msadb/modet/downloads/magma/g1000_eur`), count independent seed loci with a trans effect on gene g, test against a hypergeometric null using each gene's total trans in-degree (hub correction), score = -log10 p. Run on `temporal` dev (53 diseases; only those with >= 5 GWAS seed loci, report n) and `forward` dev.
- **Expected.** Applies to maybe 40 % of diseases (those with GWAS seed loci and blood-relevant biology). Within them: zero-support stratum AUROC 0.60-0.65, fused nuAP +0.005-0.015; overall +0.003-0.006 nuAP, AP ~0. **Kill:** within-applicable-disease fused gain < +0.005 nuAP, or hub-corrected score AUROC for new positives < 0.55.
- **Most likely failure.** Trans effects are tiny and blood-only; after hub correction few genes have >= 2 convergent loci, so the score is zero for most genes and diseases.

### CI-03 — Genome-wide cis-MR + colocalisation as the non-transfer channel
- **Mechanism.** For every gene with a cis instrument (eQTL in any GTEx v8 tissue / eQTLGen blood; cis-pQTL), run SMR/HEIDI-style MR and a colocalisation approximation against the disease's own variant-level summary statistics available at the seed date, including sub-threshold loci. Output a signed, per-gene causal posterior that exists for genes with zero disease knowledge and separates the causal gene from LD neighbours better than MAGMA's positional z.
- **p_typical:** 0.50
- **Data.** On disk: `/mnt/msadb/modet/downloads/gwas_sumstats_t0` (90 GB pre-T0), `gwas_sumstats` (post-T0, for `forward`), GTEx v8 (inventory). Download: GTEx v8 significant cis-eQTL pairs (~1.5 GB) or SMR BESD files (~30 GB all tissues), eQTLGen cis (~3 GB, https://www.eqtlgen.org/cis-eqtls.html). Open Targets ships precomputed GWAS-vs-molQTL colocalisation (COLOC-PIP + eCAVIAR; https://platform-docs.opentargets.org/credible-set, https://blog.opentargets.org/open-targets-platform-25-12-has-been-released/) for `forward`/`holdout`.
  **Leakage:** `temporal-clean` with GTEx v8 (2020) + eQTLGen + T0 sumstats. OT colocalisation tables feed L2G, i.e. they are an input of the label itself -> on `forward` use only the 25.09 tables; on `holdout` this is `holdout-current` with a direct route (coloc -> L2G -> label), must be masked per hidden pair.
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~1.5 h for 10 studies).** For the temporal dev diseases with a T0 study in `gwas_magma_t0` (list via `magma_runs_t0.tsv`): top-eQTL-per-gene-per-tissue SMR z = z_gwas(top eQTL SNP) with HEIDI skipped; aggregate over tissues by ACAT; compare against MAGMA `_w10` z as the positional control. `score` = SMR ACAT z; fused variant = rank-average with `modet_headline`.
- **Expected.** Among GWAS-only arrivals in covered diseases: AUROC 0.80 -> 0.83 over MAGMA z (brief #4 gives ~0.8 for sub-threshold z), top-100 recall of GWAS-only arrivals 16 % -> 22 %. Overall fused nuAP +0.010-0.020 in covered diseases, +0.004-0.008 across all dev diseases; AP +0.005. **Kill:** SMR-ACAT does not beat MAGMA `_w10` z by >= 0.02 AUROC on GWAS-only arrivals (then it is the already-known sub-threshold signal with extra steps).
- **Most likely failure.** Only ~1/3 of GWAS loci have an eQTL explanation; MAGMA's positional signal already captures most of what top-SNP SMR sees, and T0 sumstats exist for too few dev diseases.

### CI-04 — Directed donor graph: genes flow down the causal arrow
- **Mechanism.** Replace symmetric overlap weights in transfer by a directed disease -> disease flow matrix. Estimate direction two ways: (a) historically, from 19.04 -> 21.04 arrivals: for each ordered pair (a, d), the rate at which genes exclusive to a later appeared in d, shrunk to ontology-branch-pair priors (a Granger-style kernel); (b) genetically, where both traits have sumstats, by bidirectional MR / latent-causal-variable asymmetry on MAGMA z and the Neale rg table. Risk-factor and upstream traits donate; downstream consequences do not donate back.
- **p_typical:** 0.10
- **Data.** On disk: `modet/data/processed/groundtruth/` (OT 19.04 / 21.04 sets), `neale_rg_table/{h2_results,r2_results}`, `gwas_magma_t0`. No download.
  **Leakage:** `temporal-clean` (19.04 -> 21.04 history precedes the 21.04 -> 25.09 targets). Route: the banned-list item "provenance-restricted transfer" and "T0 UKB MAGMA studies as donors" are different (they restricted or added donors; this orients them), but family leakage is the same as for transfer, so use the harness's `fam_rows` masking unchanged. For `forward`, fit the kernel on 21.04 -> 25.09 with sealed rows removed.
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~15 min).** Fork `_transfer(tasks, B, k=50)` in `baselines.py`: `w(a->d) = jaccard(a, d) * exp(theta * asym(a, d))`, `asym = log((n_{a->d}+1)/(n_{d->a}+1))` from 19.04 -> 21.04 with branch-pair shrinkage; theta in {0, 0.5, 1, 2} chosen on confirm-free half of dev by disease-level CV. Compare with `transfer_jaccard` by paired cluster bootstrap on `temporal` dev.
- **Expected.** +0.006-0.012 nuAP over symmetric `tr_max` (0.122), mostly from demoting hub donors (cancer, broad syndromes) that receive but should not give; AP +0.003. Inside LightGBM the gain shrinks to +0.003-0.006. **Kill:** < +0.004 nuAP over `transfer_jaccard` on dev, or theta selected = 0.
- **Most likely failure.** Two years of history (19.04 -> 21.04) is too sparse per pair, shrinkage collapses to the ontology prior, and asymmetry mostly reflects size difference (already handled by Jaccard normalisation).

### CI-05 — Power-jump hazard: per-disease gating between genetics and transfer
- **Mechanism.** Model arrival as a hazard: P(pair arrives in window) = P(causal) x P(detected | effect, N_now -> N_future). Estimate per disease its position on the discovery curve (current GWAS effective N, number of loci, h2 from the Neale table, slope of loci-vs-N in the trait class) and predict the size of the coming power jump; this sets, per disease, how deep the sub-threshold own-trait list is trusted and how the genetic channel is weighted against transfer. It also yields the module cutoff (expected number of arrivals), which no current method outputs.
- **p_typical:** 0.07
- **Data.** On disk: `gwas_magma_t0` (`_w10` z, `_leads`), GWAS catalog (study N and dates; brief resources), `neale_rg/ukb31063_h2_all.02Oct2019.tsv.gz`, OT 19.04/21.04 sets for fitting the hazard on the previous window.
  **Leakage:** `temporal-clean` if N_future is forecast from pre-T0 trends only (never read the realised post-2021 N). Route: using the realised 2025 study size or the count of 25.09 credible sets as the "jump" is label leakage; forbid it in code (only catalog rows with date <= 2021-04).
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~30 min).** `score = (1 - g_d) * rank(transfer) + g_d * rank(z_w10)` with `g_d = sigmoid(a + b * log(expected new loci_d))`, expected new loci from a 2-parameter loci ~ N^alpha curve per trait class fitted on catalog history <= 2021-04, and N_future = N_now x class median growth. Fit a, b on the 19.04 -> 21.04 window. Baselines: fixed g (best constant), g = 0. Also score the predicted number of arrivals vs realised (Spearman across diseases).
- **Expected.** Over the best constant mixing weight: +0.004-0.008 nuAP, AP +0.002; GWAS-only top-100 recall 16 % -> 20 %; predicted-vs-realised arrival count Spearman >= 0.4 (a new deliverable: calibrated module size). **Kill:** gain over best constant weight < +0.003 nuAP AND arrival-count Spearman < 0.25.
- **Most likely failure.** Too few diseases with T0 sumstats to fit a per-disease gate (the gate degenerates to "has sumstats: yes/no"), and study growth is driven by funding/biobank availability that pre-T0 trends do not predict.

### CI-06 — Discovery-channel physics: detectability as a second factor
- **Mechanism.** Selection flattens common-variant effects at constrained genes, and GWAS vs burden tests systematically surface different genes (GWAS: long regulatory domains, pleiotropic; burden: trait-specific, long coding sequence). Fit an explicit detection model per evidence channel, P(detected by channel c | causal, gene architecture: s_het, coding length, cis-regulatory domain size, number of common cis-eQTL instruments, local LD/gene density), and multiply it with the disease-specific causal score rather than adding features. This predicts by which route a causal gene will surface, and stops the model from spending top-100 slots on causal genes that cannot arrive in the window.
- **p_typical:** 0.08
- **Data.** On disk: `gnomad_v2.1.1` constraint, `cs2g`, `ot_evidence/evidence_2104` (channel per pair via `sourceId`), GTEx v8, `modet/data/processed/covariates/`, MAGMA gene annotations (`chr/start/stop`, SNP counts in `gwas_magma_t0` parquet). Download optional: s_het table (Zeng et al. 2024, few MB).
  **Leakage:** `temporal-clean` (gene architecture is time-invariant; detection model fitted on <= 21.04 pairs). Route: this is partly the banned "tier-0 gene-propensity prior"; the new reason is (i) channel-specific, (ii) multiplicative with a disease-specific causal score instead of an additive column, (iii) evaluated per arrival class. If the single pooled propensity already equals it, kill.
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~30 min).** Fit 3 logistic detection models (GWAS, rare/Mendelian, other) on 21.04 pairs: outcome = channel of first evidence given the gene is in the disease set. `score = sum_c pi_dc * P_det,c(g) * causal_d(g)`, with `causal_d` = `modet_headline` score and `pi_dc` = the disease's channel mix among seeds. Compare with `modet_headline` and with headline x pooled propensity.
- **Expected.** +0.003-0.007 nuAP, AP +0.000-0.003 (nuAP gains because detection is not degree); GWAS-only top-100 recall +2-3 points. **Kill:** < +0.003 nuAP over headline x pooled propensity (the banned control).
- **Most likely failure.** LightGBM with gene covariates already learned a pooled version of the detection term; the channel split adds variance, not signal.

### CI-07 — Invariant predictors across discovery eras and channels
- **Mechanism.** Define environments = (arrival window 19.04->21.04 vs seeds-internal holdout) x (evidence channel) x (therapeutic area), and train with an invariance penalty (group-DRO / V-REx, or the simple version: keep only features whose per-environment partial effect has stable sign and rank). Ascertainment features (popularity, degree, annotation count) are predicted to be non-invariant; mechanism features invariant. The claim is that the invariant model loses a little on easy positives and gains on the strata where shortcuts fail: zero-support, unknown-to-any-disease.
- **p_typical:** 0.06
- **Data.** On disk only: modet feature tables (`modet/data/processed/features/`, `covariates/`), OT 19.04/21.04 sets and `evidence_2104` sources.
  **Leakage:** `temporal-clean`. Route: environment definition must not use the 25.09 arrival classes of the evaluated diseases (brief #5 arrival classes exist in `groundtruth/`; use them only for reporting).
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~1 h).** Train the headline LightGBM once per environment (6-8 fits), compute per-feature SHAP rank and sign per environment, drop features with rank-correlation across environments below the median, refit; second variant: LightGBM with per-environment sample reweighting to equalise worst-group loss (3 rounds). Evaluate on `temporal` dev overall + zero-support + genes-unknown-to-any-disease strata.
- **Expected.** Overall nuAP -0.003 to +0.004 (within noise, brief #7); zero-support and unknown-gene strata within-pool AUC 0.60-0.63 -> 0.64-0.66; AP -0.005 (hubs demoted). The value is diagnostic even at zero overall gain: it names which signals are ascertainment. **Kill:** stratum AUC gain < 0.02 AND overall nuAP loss > 0.005.
- **Most likely failure.** Nearly all current signal is transfer, which is itself an ascertainment-like feature that is invariant in sign everywhere, so nothing gets removed.

### CI-08 — Genetic-correlation-borrowed gene z (multi-trait posterior)
- **Mechanism.** Transfer in summary-statistic space instead of label space: the posterior own-trait gene effect is the rg-weighted conditional expectation of the gene's z across all traits with sumstats (MTAG-like, at gene level, with the trait-trait z covariance estimated from null genes to remove sample overlap). A gene with z = 2.5 in three genetically correlated traits and no disease annotation anywhere becomes reachable. Under-powered and rare diseases borrow power from well-powered correlated quantitative traits.
- **p_typical:** 0.30
- **Data.** On disk: `modet/data/processed/gwas_magma_t0` (~108 studies; pre-T0), `gwas_magma` (102 EFO, post-T0, for `forward`), `neale_rg_table`.
  **Leakage:** `temporal-clean` on `temporal` with the T0 set only. Route: the banned "T0 UKB MAGMA studies as donors" used studies as extra donor rows in label-overlap transfer; this conditions in z-space with an estimated covariance and needs no seed overlap. If its score correlates > 0.9 with that earlier donor variant, treat as already explored.
- **Minimal test (CPU, ~20 min).** Build Z (genes x ~108 studies) from `_w10`; Sigma = shrunk correlation of Z over the bottom-80 % |z| genes; for a disease with its own study t: `zhat_t = Sigma_{t,-t} Sigma_{-t,-t}^{-1} Z_{-t}` and score = z_t + lambda * zhat_t; for a disease without a study: weights from regressing the seed indicator on Z (ridge, seeds only, gene-level leave-locus-out). Compare with own-trait `_w10` z and with `modet_headline`.
- **Expected.** GWAS-only arrival AUROC ~0.80 -> 0.83 where own study exists; opens the genetic channel for diseases without a study (AUROC ~0.65 for their GWAS-only arrivals). Fused nuAP +0.006-0.012, AP +0.004. **Kill:** < +0.005 nuAP fused on dev, or no gain over own-trait z in covered diseases and AUROC < 0.60 in uncovered ones.
- **Most likely failure.** The ~108 T0 studies cluster in a few trait families; for most dev diseases no correlated well-powered trait exists and the ridge weights fit locus structure of the seeds.

### CI-09 — Allelic-series concordance: direction as evidence
- **Mechanism.** Score a gene-disease pair by agreement in direction and dose between independent instruments: rare LoF burden beta, missense burden, common-variant eQTL-MR sign, and (where available) drug mechanism direction. Sub-threshold burden statistics (p ~1e-3) that agree in sign with the common-variant MR estimate are promoted; discordant or common-only signals in dense LD are demoted. This attacks the non-GWAS arrival channel and gives each module gene a sign (risk-increasing vs protective when reduced), i.e. internal module structure.
- **p_typical:** 0.35
- **Data.** Download: Genebass gene-burden results, 4,529 phenotypes x 394,841 exomes (https://app.genebass.org/downloads; Hail tables, order of 100 GB total, per-phenotype exports small; paper https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9903662); AZ PheWAS portal as alternative. On disk: `minikel2024`, `ot_chembl_2104/2509`, CI-03 outputs.
  **Leakage:** Genebass (2022) and AZ PheWAS (2021-08) are after T0 -> `transductive` on `temporal`; clean on `forward` (labels 25.09 -> 26.09). Route on `forward`: OT ingests gene-burden evidence from these same sources, so a strong burden hit is already a 25.09 seed, not a target; only the sub-threshold tail is informative, which is exactly the test.
- **Minimal test (~1.5 h, mostly download of 20-30 phenotype exports).** Map `forward` dev diseases to Genebass phecodes/ICD (string + EFO xref), take pLoF burden z per gene, `score = |z_burden|` and the concordance variant `z_burden * sign(z_SMR)`; fuse with `modet_headline`; report by arrival evidence source.
- **Expected.** On `forward` in mapped diseases: fused nuAP +0.005-0.010, concentrated in non-GWAS arrivals (gene-burden and ClinVar channels); AP +0.003. **Kill:** sub-threshold burden z (after removing all pairs already in 25.09) AUROC < 0.56 for new positives.
- **Most likely failure.** Binary-disease burden tests in UKB are badly under-powered (few cases); the sub-threshold tail is noise except for quantitative traits, which are not our tasks.

## Bets
- **Would bet on: CI-03 + CI-08 as one "own-trait causal genetics" channel** (cis-MR/coloc and rg-borrowed z). It is the only route in this list that directly addresses the largest hard stratum (57 % GWAS-only arrivals, 16 % found; brief #4) with a signal already measured at AUC ~0.8, and it scores genes unknown to any disease. It is not the most original idea here, which is why CI-05 should ride on it: the gate turns a feature into a per-disease decision and delivers a module cutoff.
- **Most underrated: CI-02 (trans-QTL convergence)**, with CI-01 as its experimental twin. Both are directed, germline- or intervention-anchored edges into the core, which is the one kind of "network" modet never tested (everything measured in brief #2 is undirected co-membership). If either moves the zero-support stratum from 0.4 % to a few percent top-100 recall, that is a qualitatively new capability even with a flat overall nuAP.
- Cheapest informative experiment overall: CI-04 (15 min, no download, temporal-clean).
